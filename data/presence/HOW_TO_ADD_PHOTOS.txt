HOW TO ADD PHOTOS FOR THE COMPONENT PRESENCE CHECK
===================================================
(LG Indicator, red board, SMT stage. See planner.md for the why.)


1. FOLDER STRUCTURE
-------------------

data\presence\
    golden\red\                                  3 careful photos of ONE good red board (the golden reference)

    raw\red\good\                                ALL good boards in ONE folder, NO text file.
        R02_01.jpg  R02_02.jpg ... R02_06.jpg      file name starts with the board ID
        R03_01.jpg  R03_02.jpg ...                 (a sub-folder per board, raw\red\good\R02\, works too)

    raw\red\removed\R01\                         a board with missing parts: ~6 photos
    raw\red\removed\R01\R01_missing.txt            + the missing-parts file

    raw\red\removed\R21\step1\                   a board you desolder step by step: one folder per step
    raw\red\removed\R21\step1\R21_step1_missing.txt   (empty file, or no file: nothing missing yet)
    raw\red\removed\R21\step2\                     photos after the 1st removal
    raw\red\removed\R21\step2\R21_step2_missing.txt   parts missing in THESE photos
    raw\red\removed\R21\step3\R21_step3_missing.txt   ...

    raw\red\bare\R31\                            unpopulated PCBs (no parts at all), NO text file needed
    test\red\good\R41\                           TEST boards: other boards, photographed LAST, never used for training
    test\red\removed\R45\R45_missing.txt         test board with missing parts

Board IDs: write a number on the BACK of every board with a marker (R01, R02, ... R = red).
Why the ID matters even for good boards: the 6 photos of one board are almost the same picture. The program
uses the ID to keep all photos of a board together - all in training, or all in the check set - otherwise
the model is tested on a board it has already seen and the result looks better than it is.
A board is in good OR removed OR test, never in two of them.
Photo file names: good boards: <board ID>_<number>.jpg (R02_01.jpg ...). Boards in their own folder
(removed, test): keep the names the phone gives. Never edit the picture itself.

NOTE 2026-10-09 - what the first data turned out to be: the good photos R_01 ... R_43 carry only a photo number, no board ID.
The program recognised from the pictures themselves that they are only 6 boards (R_01-07, R_08-14, R_15-21, R_22-28,
R_29-37, R_38-43; see ml\presence\board_groups.json, where they are called G01 ... G06) and that none of them is one of the
removed boards m_01 ... m_04. So the data holds 10 different boards, not 47. Do not rename these photos.
For NEW good boards use ONE ID per physical board, the same on all of its photos, e.g. G07_01.jpg, G07_02.jpg (board G07),
then G08_01.jpg ... A new board counts as ONE board however many photos it has: 7 more different boards help the model far
more than 70 more photos of the boards that are already here.


2. THE MISSING-PARTS FILE
-------------------------

RULE: every folder of photos that shows missing parts gets ONE text file in the SAME folder as the photos:

    name:      <board id>_missing.txt               e.g.  R01_missing.txt
               <board id>_<step>_missing.txt        e.g.  R21_step2_missing.txt   (step-by-step boards)
    content:   the names of ALL parts that are missing in the photos of that folder,
               separated by commas (spaces and new lines are fine too)

Example: raw\red\removed\R01\R01_missing.txt

    Q9, R12

Example: raw\red\removed\R21\step3\R21_step3_missing.txt  (everything missing at step 3, also the parts
removed at step 2)

    R12, C14, Q4, D11, R31,
    U1, R2K2_5, C100N_3, D4148_6, R45

Rules:
- Upper / lower case does not matter (q9 = Q9). Use the names of the list in section 3.
- NEVER list R50, R32, Q11, D14 (never fitted in this variant), R43, R44, D16, C17, LEDs, SW1, SW2:
  they are not checked. A line starting with # is a comment.
- Good boards: no file. Bare boards (no parts at all): no file, the folder says it.
- If a part is only half removed (tilted, standing on one end, hanging on one pad), finish removing it before
  you take the photos - for now we teach only "present" and "missing".
- If you are not sure which part is missing, do not use that board.


3. THE 86 PART NAMES THAT ARE CHECKED
-------------------------------------

Resistors (printed on the board):
    R1, R2, R3, R4, R9, R10, R11, R12, R13, R18, R19, R21, R22, R23, R24, R25, R26, R27, R28, R29,
    R30, R31, R34, R35, R36, R37, R38, R39, R40, R42, R45, R48, R49, R51, R52, R53, R54, R55, R56,
    R57, R58, R59, R60
Capacitors:   C2, C14, C16
Diodes:       D1, D11, D12, D13 (D13 = the MB10S bridge)
Transistors:  Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5, Q6, Q7, Q8, Q9, Q10
ICs:          U1 (78M05 regulator), U2 (microcontroller)

The three unlabelled rows under the transistors (labels "R_2K2", "100nF", "D_4148" on the left):
    R2K2_1 ... R2K2_8      the "222" resistor row
    C100N_1 ... C100N_8    the tan capacitor row
    D4148_1 ... D4148_8    the "1F4" diode row
    Numbering: hold the board like the golden photo (header pins at the BOTTOM).
    _1 is the LEFT-most one (under Q2), _8 the RIGHT-most one (under Q1):
        column:   1    2    3    4    5    6    7    8
        above:    Q2   Q3   Q4   Q8   Q7   Q6   Q5   Q1

NOT checked (never write them in missing.txt):
    R50, R32, Q11, D14         never fitted in this variant
    R43, R44, D16, C17         not fitted on the golden (to be confirmed)
    all LEDs, SW1, SW2, the header, the holes   (empty at the SMT stage)


4. HOW EVERY PHOTO MUST LOOK
----------------------------

- DO NOT CROP. Keep the background around the board. All four board edges must be inside the photo with
  a little background around them (about 1 - 3 cm). The program finds the board edges itself; if the
  photo is cut exactly at the board edge (or cuts into it), the board cannot be found reliably.
- Do not rotate, edit, filter, compress or resize the photos. Save the original from the phone.
- The board fills about 60 - 80 % of the photo (phone 20 - 30 cm above the board), straight from above.
- Plain background that is NOT red, blue or green: white paper, a grey table or black cloth.
- No flash. No lamp shining straight on the board (reflections hide parts). Daylight or room light.
- Tap on the board to focus, hold the phone still, the photo must be sharp.
- Camera: the in-app camera of our app, or the phone camera at full resolution, 1x (no zoom), no filter,
  no portrait / beauty mode.
- Vary between the ~6 photos of a board: turn the board (0 / 90 / 180 / 270 degrees), a slightly different
  height, a different place in the photo, a different light (day / evening).
- Golden photos (golden\red\): the same rules, but take the 3 best ones you can: sharp, even light, straight.
